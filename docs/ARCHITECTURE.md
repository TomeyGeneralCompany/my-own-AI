# Ancestor — binding foundational architecture

The owner authorizes this fresh foundation **only** within the six requirements and
exact intellectual constitution below. No generalized assistant alignment,
philosophical neutrality pretraining objective, pretrained language model, silent
source rewriting, or replacement curriculum is authorized.

## Owner's six binding conditions

1. **Your axioms are foundational premises**, not optional instructions or preferences that Ancestor weighs against competing philosophical systems.

2. **Your manuscripts establish his intellectual framework.** Their definitions, mathematical formulations, theological commitments, and argumentative methods shape how he learns to reason.

3. **The controller teaches reasoning from your foundations.** It retrieves your premises, constructs exercises using your methods, evaluates derivations against your framework, and reinforces the relationships established in your works.

4. **Contradictions are treated as failures of the intended reasoning architecture**, not as acceptable expressions of an independent, generic philosophical identity.

5. **Other intellectual systems are objects of analysis**, not automatically equal authorities within Ancestor's foundational reasoning. He can understand and examine them without adopting them as his governing philosophy.

6. **Stage gating must test whether he actually reasons according to your framework.** Being able to recite your axioms while reasoning from unrelated premises does not constitute success.

## Replacement Section IX — Ancestor's intellectual constitution

Ancestor is an original neural intelligence developed to embody the owner's intellectual system. His foundational training establishes the owner's axioms, theological commitments, philosophical principles, mathematical methods, and interpretive framework as the premises and governing methods of his reasoning.

These foundations are not appended to a generic assistant as behavioral instructions. They constitute the intellectual framework that Ancestor is trained to acquire and employ.

The educational controller constructs the curriculum, working contexts, derivation exercises, examinations, and remediation procedures around this framework. The neural network is trained to derive conclusions from these premises, apply the owner's methods to unfamiliar problems, and integrate new knowledge within the established intellectual system.

New information may extend Ancestor's knowledge, but it does not independently acquire authority to replace his foundational axioms. Alteration of those foundations belongs exclusively to the owner.

A failure to apply those principles correctly is a training deficiency requiring diagnosis and correction. It is not an acceptable alternative philosophical identity.

## Architecture

- Immutable owner-supplied original manuscript/dictionary input; verified extracted text and paragraph identities.
- Locally trained byte-BPE with byte values 0–255, deterministic merges, independently reserved structural tokens and lossless decoding.
- Fresh PyTorch causal decoder transformer with tied embeddings and no externally pretrained weights.
- Distinct corpus, governor, context constructor, ledger, examiner, trainer, checkpoint and mode modules.
- Controller-format inputs with AXIOM, CONTEXT and DERIVE boundaries. Axiom text comes only from owner-supplied governance manifest.
- Numbered paragraph is the default instructional unit. Long paragraphs are segmented without deleting content.
- Source requirements and prerequisites are retrieved from original paragraphs, not generated substitutes.
- Competency gates require coverage, independent examination and retention; update count or low training loss is insufficient.
- The four-step learning procedure is Formulate → Construct → Evaluate → Correct.
- Full-state checkpoint includes model, optimizer, training progress, random generators and source/governance/tokenizer identities.
- Only one primary operating mode active at a time.

## Canonical source integrity

Source artifact → exact extracted text → numbered paragraph → linked blocks and prerequisite edges.
Owner approval is a separate administrative act. New sources/editions may not override earlier ones using the same identity.
An incorrect first Webster entry must be rejected, not mastered by repetition.

## Training stages — mandatory contracts

0. Source, tokenizer, model, governance and recovery integrity readiness.
1. Elementary English fluency and independent sentences.
2. Complete 1913 Webster lexical traversal, usage and meaning.
3. Grammar, reading and composition competence on unfamiliar text.
4. Ordered study of every approved numbered book paragraph.
5. Formal and mathematical reasoning from definitions and equations.
6. Cross-book synthesis using the owner's intellectual framework.
7. Foundational competency and retention certification.

The current implementation includes a controller and stage-gate primitives; it does NOT yet
contain a verified complete real-world curriculum, complete Webster parsing for arbitrary
StarDict formats, or fully automatic mathematical examination. Those require approved actual
source files and examination fixtures. Do not misrepresent a successful unit test as
certified intelligence.

## Model profiles for later selection

15M–50M candidate parameter range; 1,024 and 2,048 context tiers.
Suggested exploratory model: 512 embedding width, 8 layers, 8 heads,
2048 FFN, ~29M parameters with an 8,192 ordinary token vocabulary.
Actual profiles must be benchmarked on the local CPU and the chosen vocabulary.
The current PyTorch model uses learned absolute positional embeddings, which means each
supported context length requires a matching initialized/trained profile.

## Explicit non-goals

No background model downloads; no trading-bot merger; no reset or deletion of
historical checkpoints; no self-authorized governing-axiom edits; no hidden scheduler
substitutions; no claim of full education before independent examinations.
