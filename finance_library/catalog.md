# Finance reference acquisition catalog

This catalog is a discovery aid, not imported textbooks or approved training data. Acquire the source documents separately using their applicable permissions, preserve publisher and attribution, and have the owner approve the intended use. Dates and legal requirements must be checked against current authoritative texts. External literature is evidence, not philosophical authority.

## Foundations
- OpenStax, Principles of Economics 3e: https://assets.openstax.org/oscms-prodcms/media/documents/Economics3e-WEB_AObkTqf.pdf — supply and demand, elasticity, production, markets, incentives, macroeconomics, monetary and fiscal systems, international exchange.
- OpenStax, Principles of Finance: https://assets.openstax.org/oscms-prodcms/media/documents/PrinciplesofFinance-WEB.pdf — financial statements, time value, valuation, return measurement, working capital, financing and investment.
- MIT OpenCourseWare, Finance Theory I (15.401): https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-401-finance-theory-i-fall-2008/ — valuation, equities, bonds, forwards, futures, options, portfolio mathematics; exercises and examinations.

## Market mechanics and microstructure
- SEC Investor.gov, Types of Orders: https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/how-stock-markets-work/types-orders — market, limit and stop order definitions.
- SEC Investor.gov, Understanding Order Types: https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-14 — order execution, timing and liquidity; educational bulletin, not binding rule text.
- Cont, Stoikov and Talreja (2010), A Stochastic Model for Order Book Dynamics, DOI 10.1287/opre.1090.0780: https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/opre.1090.0780 — stochastic order arrivals, short-horizon price events and order-book dynamics; publisher rights apply.

## Further acquisition topics
Source review is required before incorporating additional material. Seek econometrics, probability, statistics, stochastic processes, time series, numerical methods, optimization, market microstructure, quote and trade data formats, spreads, depth, liquidity, queueing, tick sizes, execution costs, slippage, latency, settlement, FX, cryptocurrency, derivatives, fractional instruments and point-in-time historical datasets. Include short-horizon research, competing hypotheses and independently reproducible empirical studies. Acquire official exchange/provider specifications and current primary legal texts separately; record publication and effective dates.

## Curriculum and qualification
Order competencies: economic vocabulary and accounting; financial mathematics; instrument mechanics; statistical inference; microstructure and data interpretation; research methodology and experimental evaluation; objective-based practical examination. Review exercises and answer keys separately. Train only on owner-approved materials after foundation qualification; keep held-out exercises out of optimization. Do not infer live trading competency from recall, historical examples or synthetic fixtures.
